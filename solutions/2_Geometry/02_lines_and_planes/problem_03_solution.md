# Exercise 3. Plane Through a Point with a Given Normal Vector

## Question

Find the equation of the plane through $P=(1,-2,3)$ and perpendicular to $n=(2,1,-1)$. Check the result by substituting the point $P$ and explain the role of the normal vector.

## Solution

For any point $X=(x,y,z)$ on the plane, the vector $X-P$ is perpendicular to $n$. Hence

$$
n\cdot(X-P)=0.
$$

Substituting the given point and normal gives

$$
2(x-1)+(y+2)-(z-3)=0.
$$

After expansion, the plane is

$$
2x+y-z+3=0.
$$

The point check is

$$
2(1)+(-2)-3+3=0.
$$

The nonzero normal vector $n=(2,1,-1)$ fixes the orientation of the plane: it is perpendicular to every direction lying in the plane. The condition that the plane passes through $P$ fixes its position.
