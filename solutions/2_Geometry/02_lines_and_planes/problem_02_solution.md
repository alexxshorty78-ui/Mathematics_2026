# Exercise 2. A Line in Space

## Question

Given

$$
L:(x,y,z)=(2,-1,3)+t(1,2,-2).
$$

State a point on the line and its direction vector, check whether $P=(4,3,-1)$ lies on $L$, and find the point corresponding to $t=-1$.

## Solution

The parametric form identifies the point $A=(2,-1,3)$, obtained at $t=0$, and the direction vector $v=(1,2,-2)$. Its coordinate equations are

$$
\begin{cases}
x=2+t, \\
y=-1+2t, \\
z=3-2t. \\
\end{cases}
$$

For $P$, the first coordinate requires $2+t=4$, so $t=2$. The same parameter gives

$$
-1+2(2)=3,\qquad 3-2(2)=-1.
$$

All three coordinates agree, so $P\in L$.

At $t=-1$ the point is

$$
(2,-1,3)-(1,2,-2)=(1,-3,5).
$$
