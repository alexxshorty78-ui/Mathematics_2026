# Exercise 1. Determinant of a 2×2 Matrix

## Question

Compute

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -2 \\
5 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 7 \\
2 & 14 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Which matrix is invertible? Justify your answer.

## Solution

For a $2\times2$ matrix, the determinant is $ad-bc$.

For the first matrix,

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -2 \\
5 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix}
=3\cdot4-(-2)\cdot5=12+10=22.
$$

Since its determinant is nonzero, the first matrix is invertible.

For the second matrix,

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 7 \\
2 & 14 \\
\end{pmatrix}
=1\cdot14-7\cdot2=14-14=0.
$$

Since its determinant is zero, the second matrix is not invertible.
