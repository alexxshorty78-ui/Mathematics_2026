# Exercise 6. Parameter and Invertibility

## Question

For

$$
A(t)=
\begin{pmatrix}
t & 1 \\
2 & t \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

find all values of $t$ for which the matrix is not invertible. Explain how your answer is related to the determinant being zero.

## Solution

The determinant is

$$
\det A(t)=t\cdot t-1\cdot2=t^2-2.
$$

A square matrix is not invertible exactly when its determinant is zero. Set the determinant equal to zero:

$$
t^2-2=0.
$$

Therefore,

$$
t=\pm\sqrt{2}.
$$

The matrix is not invertible for $t=\sqrt{2}$ and $t=-\sqrt{2}$, which are exactly the values that make its determinant zero.
