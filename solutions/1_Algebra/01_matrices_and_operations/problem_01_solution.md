### Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

Given

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
-2 & 3 \\
4 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

#### 1. Sizes of matrices $A$ and $B$

Matrix $A$ has 2 rows and 3 columns, so its size is $2 \times 3$.

Matrix $B$ has 3 rows and 2 columns, so its size is $3 \times 2$.

#### 2. Matrix entries

- $a_{12}$ is the entry in the 1st row and 2nd column of $A$: $a_{12} = -1$
- $a_{23}$ is the entry in the 2nd row and 3rd column of $A$: $a_{23} = 5$
- $b_{21}$ is the entry in the 2nd row and 1st column of $B$: $b_{21} = -2$
- $b_{32}$ is the entry in the 3rd row and 2nd column of $B$: $b_{32} = 1$

#### 3. Row and column vectors

The second row of $A$ as a row vector:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 4 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

The first column of $B$ as a column vector:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
-2 \\
4 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$
