# Exercise 9. A System from a Sum Condition

## Question

Find numbers $x,y$ satisfying

$$
x+y=10,\qquad 2x+5y=32.
$$

Solve the system and explain geometrically why the solution is unique.

## Solution

Subtract twice the first equation from the second:

$$
(2x+5y)-2(x+y)=32-2(10),
$$

so $3y=12$ and $y=4$. Then $x=10-4=6$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=(6,4)}.
$$

**Check:**

$$
6+4=10,
\qquad
2(6)+5(4)=12+20=32.
$$

Geometrically, the equations describe the lines

$$
y=10-x,
\qquad
y=\frac{32}{5}-\frac25x.
$$

Their slopes are $-1$ and $-2/5$, which are different. Hence the lines intersect at exactly one point, $(6,4)$, and the solution is unique.
